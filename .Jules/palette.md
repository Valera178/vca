## 2024-05-18 - Interactive Container Accessibility Pattern
**Learning:** Large interactive list items (like the task cards in StudentCabinet) frequently use `div` click handlers for better UX target area, but this pattern misses keyboard support, `aria-pressed` states, and often incorrectly nests visual-only controls (like checkboxes as `<button>`) leading to screen reader confusion and nested interactive element issues.
**Action:** When implementing large clickable cards/list items, always apply `role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}`, `onKeyDown` for Space/Enter, `aria-pressed`/`aria-expanded`, and convert internal visual toggles to `aria-hidden="true"` `div`s. Ensure any genuinely interactive nested actions (like "Solve" buttons) have `e.stopPropagation()` for both click and keydown.
