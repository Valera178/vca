## 2024-05-06 - Accessible Interactive Task Cards
**Learning:** When making a large container (like a task card) interactive by adding `onClick` and converting it to a pseudo-button, it's crucial to remove inner non-functional `<button>` elements (like visual checkmarks) by converting them to `div aria-hidden="true"` to prevent screen reader confusion over nested interactive elements. Conversely, for genuinely distinct actions inside the card (like a "Solve" button), we must retain their button semantics and add `e.stopPropagation()` on both `onClick` and `onKeyDown` to prevent the parent container's toggle action from firing simultaneously.
**Action:** Always audit large interactive cards for nested buttons. Convert purely visual controls to non-interactive elements, and explicitly isolate genuinely distinct interactive children from the parent's event listeners.
